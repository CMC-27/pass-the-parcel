## The defect

`scripts/wiki_lint_core.py` `list_field()` (the flat front-matter reader) scans a block-list forward, appending `^\s+-\s*(.+)$` matches, and **breaks at the first line that is neither an item nor blank**:

```
for nxt in lines[i + 1:]:
    sub = re.match(r"^\s+-\s*(.+)$", nxt)
    if sub:            tokens.append(...)
    elif not nxt.strip(): continue
    else:              break          # <-- a YAML comment lands here
```

A YAML comment is legal anywhere in a block-list. `list_field` reads it as **end of list**. Every declared path after it is dropped — silently, with no error and no warning.

## Observed consequences (both reproduced, not theorised)

`T1-E2.11`'s declaration carried a recorded Phase-2 amendment as a comment *inside* the list. Measured on that real plan:

| Reader | Saw | Should have seen |
|---|---|---|
| `list_field(block, "touches")` | **11** paths | 16 |

The five dropped were `.devops/rules/process-lessons.md`, `.devops/sync-manifest.yaml`, `.devops/logs/version-history.md`, `.devops/logs/agent-changelog.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`. Two live effects followed:

1. **`scripts/write_set_check.py` — the Claim Protocol's *decisive* witness (step 2) — reported `exit 0` on the truncated set.** A false green on the one check whose entire job is to stop a wrong `touches` declaration before work starts. It cannot detect this class: a closure-driven witness never mines the plan body, so an undeclared-but-mandated write is invisible to it (which is why `T1-E2.11`'s own review, not the gate, caught an undeclared `REFACTORING.md`).
2. **`scripts/sprint_eligible.py` shares the reader**, so the **lane classifier and the `touches`-overlap eligibility predicate** saw **zero reserved surfaces** where the real set held three (`sync-manifest.yaml`, `version-history.md`, `agent-changelog.md`) — classifying a **serial**-lane plan as **parallel**. Overlap is the predicate that prevents two writers corrupting a shared surface; a comment could hide an overlap.

`T1-E2.11` mitigated *its own* symptom by moving every amendment rationale above the `touches:` key. That is a workaround on one plan, not a fix.

## Fix direction (not decided)

Add a fixture pair first (the `[2026-09-25]` probe rule: a check never observed failing is unproven), then either (a) make `list_field` skip comment lines rather than treat them as a terminator — the narrow, correct fix, since a comment is not a list terminator in YAML; or (b) fail loudly on a comment inside a block-list. (a) is preferred: the parser's job is to read what YAML means. Whatever ships needs a `scripts/tests/` fixture driving both readers over a commented list, and a re-check that no live plan silently depends on the current truncation.

## Why LATER

Real, deterministic, and cheap — but narrow: it needs a comment in the right place to bite, and the two plans on the trunk are already clean. The value is that it is the *second* silent-integrity gap found in the claim path this month (the first being the witness's inability to see an undeclared path), and both are the same shape: a tool that cannot fail on the input it most needs to reject.
