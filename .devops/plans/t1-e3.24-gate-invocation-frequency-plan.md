## Findings

The guardrails are mostly well-placed; the cost is **frequency stacking** — the same invariant is re-verified at up to four layers (per-plan Phase 9 -> batch wrap-up -> pre-push -> CI), and the lower layers re-check state their own diff cannot have changed. Measured for one 5-plan batch: ~70-80 mandated local invocations, of which roughly a third guard the change. *Counts are read off the skills' own mandates, not instrumented; the `.ps1`/Python gates are <1s each, so the real tax is per-invocation token + attention cost and the interruption-debris state each gate creates.*

## The principle to land (canon: `plan-lifecycle.md` § Gate Invocation Hygiene)

> **A gate runs where its subject changes** — never at a layer whose diff cannot have changed it. Per-plan gates guard the plan's diff; batch gates guard the batch's aggregate (including the wiki the wrap-up itself edits, and the counter); CI guards the repo as shipped. One home, **one invocation moment** — the frequency-space form of Managed Simplicity (`rule_fanout.py` polices where a rule is *stated*; nothing polices how often a gate is *run*).
