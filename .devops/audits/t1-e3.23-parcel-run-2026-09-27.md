## Lessons

1. **A report-only instrument must be re-run on your own diff, by a reader.** The parcel that repaired the surface-budget registry tripped it (`context-isolated` **7 sites / 1 unauthorised** against the **6 / 0** baseline) and nothing failed, because `rule_fanout.py` is report-only by contract and always exits `0`. It surfaced only because the orchestrator read the report during verification. Routed to `.devops/rules/process-lessons.md`.
2. **The pipeline reproduced the failure classes it exists to fix, and its own gates caught them** — a wrong byte literal, a lookalike probe, an under-declared cascade. The strongest available evidence that the probe contract (I2) earns its keep.
3. **Hand-run write-set derivation still found 12 → 24 gaps — on the parcel that builds the tool for it.** The mechanism is the fix; the discipline is not. The amended claim was the *fourth* recorded under-declaration in two sprints.
