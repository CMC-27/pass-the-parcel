## What the fix does (authored, proven at the satellite)

New **Step 5 — Write the Business Report** in `@sprint-close`, before archiving:

- Writes `business-report.md` into the sprint folder, so Step 6's folder move carries it into `.devops/archive/sprints/sprint-{n}-<slug>/` automatically — no plan-lifecycle rule change needed.
- Audience: business users / practitioners, not developers. Grouped per **outcome/epic/theme**, never per parcel code.
- Reusable template: goal, deliverables by theme, wins, quality paragraph, non-development open items (operator actions), what's next.
- Tone rules: benefit language; only trust-building numbers (delivered totals, test counts, capacity %) — machinery thresholds stay out; machinery vocabulary (parcel, Gate D, Kill List, spaghetti scan) prohibited.
- Operator show-and-tell before archiving; the `SPRINTS.md` register row links the report.
