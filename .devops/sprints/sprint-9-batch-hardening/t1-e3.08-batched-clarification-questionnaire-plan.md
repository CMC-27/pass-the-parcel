## 1 Phase 1: Expansion & Scoping
**Skill Executed:** `ptp-context-hunter`
**Intent:**
Present Phase 3's clarification questions as one batched questionnaire — the whole decision surface in one sitting, recommended defaults first — instead of one prompt at a time, while keeping the final validation a separate confirm.

**In Scope:**
- `ptp-context-hunter` § 3: turn "One Question At A Time (non-negotiable)" into a **mode**, batched by default where the surface supports it.
- Surface-capability handling: the opencode `question` tool takes an array; the VS Code `vscode_askQuestions` surface needs a capability check with a per-question fallback.
- Template Phase 3 note; the final `"Is this all the context required?"` stays a standalone confirm.
- A recorded rationale in `.devops/rules/process-lessons.md` — this deliberately reverses a stated non-negotiable.

**Out of Scope:**
- The AUTO/batch path: `ptp-parcel-fast` never calls the ask-tool and `ptp-phase3-answerer` is unchanged.
- Front-loading questions into `@sprint-plan` so the batch benefits (explicitly deferred, not this parcel).
- The 5–8 question budget and the Phase 10 overflow route.

**Perimeter Notes:**
- Batching trades reflection time for round-trips. The recommended-default-first ordering and the retained final validation are what keep it safe rather than merely faster.
- The batched questionnaire helps interactive `parcel` runs only; it does not change how a sprint batch answers questions.
