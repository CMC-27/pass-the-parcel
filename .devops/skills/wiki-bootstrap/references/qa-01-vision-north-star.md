# Q&A: 01 — Vision & North Star (The Goal)

**Why this doc matters to AI agents:**
Agents use this to decide what to build, what NOT to build, and how to prioritize trade-offs. A vague vision leads to features that don't fit; a stale vision leads to work against the product's actual direction.

**Required sections:**
- One-paragraph vision statement
- North star metric (single measurable number)
- The "Magic Moment" — the instant a user realizes the product's value
- Problem/solution framing (what pain exists, what we do about it)
- Competitive positioning (what makes this different)

## Questions to ask
1. In one sentence, what is the product's vision? (What does success look like for users 3 years from now?)
2. What is the single north star metric the team optimizes for?
3. What is the "Magic Moment" — the specific instant a user goes from "trying it" to "I need this"?
4. What core pain does the product solve, and for whom?
5. What is the competitive positioning — why would someone choose this over the alternatives?
6. Are there any features the team has explicitly said NO to that should be captured here as non-goals?
