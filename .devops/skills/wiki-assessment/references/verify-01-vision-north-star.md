# Verify: 01 — Vision & North Star (The Goal)

**Why this doc matters to AI agents:**
Agents use this to decide what to build, what NOT to build, and how to prioritize. A stale vision = work against the product's actual direction.

**Required sections (sanity check):**
- One-paragraph vision statement
- North star metric
- "Magic Moment"
- Problem/solution framing
- Competitive positioning

## Questions to ask
1. Does the vision statement still describe what the product actually does today?
2. Has the "Magic Moment" drifted as features were added or removed?
3. Does the north star metric still align with current business goals, or has the team pivoted?
4. Are the listed non-goals still actually non-goals? (Or have some become goals?)
5. Has the competitive landscape changed such that the positioning is now misleading?

## What to verify against
- `.devops/backlog/backlog-index.md` — current priorities
- `.devops/plans/` — active plans reveal current strategic direction
- `docs/wiki/features/` — actual shipped features vs. vision
- Recent `git log` on product-defining files
