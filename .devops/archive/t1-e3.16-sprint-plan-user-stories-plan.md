## Theme-Epic: T1 - Parcel Pipeline Machinery, E3 - Concurrency & Sprint Lifecycle

## Findings

| # | Gap | Evidence | Impact |
|---|-----|----------|--------|
| 1 | **Sprint planning commits plans without a user-facing story.** `@sprint-plan` §4 commits triaged backlog plans into the queue; the plans carry acceptance criteria but no "as a user/owner I…" framing, and nothing downstream consumes a story. | `sprint-plan/SKILL.md`; `plans/template-plan.md`. | Acceptance criteria are written developer-ward; the product owner's manual tests (T1-E3.15) are then derived from them rather than from intent. |

## What the fix does (operator-authored intent)

Close the loop **story → dev → user test → report**:

1. **`@sprint-plan` Step:** for each committed plan, draft one or more short user stories. Where a plan already carries acceptance criteria, the story is derived from them; branches checking a story gain a `story:`/`stories:` front-matter row.
2. **`template-plan.md`:** Phase 4 (requirements) gains the story as an input the acceptance criteria should trace to — criteria checked against the story, not just the mechanics.
3. **Chain to T1-E3.15:** sprint-close's test queue is derived from the stories + criteria, so what the operator tests is exactly what was promised; the business report (T1-E3.12) then narrates outcomes against stories, not parcel codes.

## What a fix touches

`.devops/skills/sprint-plan/SKILL.md` (commit-time story drafting), `.devops/plans/template-plan.md` (Phase 4 story input), `.devops/skills/sprint-close/SKILL.md` (query the story field when building the test queue — cite, one line). Per-skill version bumps.
