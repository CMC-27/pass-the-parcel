**Q1 — Auto-Resolution:** **Authoritative.** The host runs the script, acts only on its JSON, and on **any non-zero exit HALTS the batch** — it never falls back to reasoning from prose.
**Rationale:** The prose predicate is exactly what has no executable test (the `G2` class defect); a fallback re-installs the untested dialect as the operative one precisely when the tested one fails, and `ptp-parcel-fast` § Per-plan outcome map already treats an unproven gate as a stop-the-line rather than a quality judgement. A halt is recoverable (`sprint-run` § 5 resume path); a silent prose recomputation is not auditable.
**Source:** `.devops/skills/sprint-run/SKILL.md` § 5 + § 2 | `.devops/agents/parcel-sprint.agent.md` § Workflow step 1, § Hard halts | Sprint 9 `sprint.md` § Risks (*"halt on a script error (fallback to prose is explicitly forbidden)"*)

**Q2 — Auto-Resolution:** **Zero-arg scan by default** — resolve the ACTIVE sprint from `.devops/backlog/SPRINTS.md`, then read the sprint folder (queue), `.devops/plans/` (claimed, template excluded) and `.devops/archive/` — **plus two explicit overrides:** `--root <dir>` (fixture trees) and `--sprint-dir <dir>` (a sprint that is not yet `🟢 ACTIVE`).
**Rationale:** A single deterministic command removes the host's need to derive paths from a markdown table — that derivation is itself an untested predicate — while the overrides are load-bearing: the fixture test builds temp trees via `--root`, and `@sprint-plan` § 4b's commit-time preflight runs *before* a sprint is ACTIVE, so `--sprint-dir` is what makes the predicate callable there when a later wave wires it. 0 or >1 ACTIVE rows is an **error** (non-zero exit), matching `sprint-run` § 1 step 1's halt-and-ask rule rather than guessing.
