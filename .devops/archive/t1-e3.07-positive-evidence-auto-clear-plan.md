# T1-E3.07 - Positive-evidence auto-clear for AUTO gates

> **Origin:** AUTO gates clear on the *absence* of a rejection — an empty or vague self-review passes by omission. This parcel replaces that negative test with a positive-evidence contract and makes an unproven gate a stop-the-line. Wave 1 of the batch-hardening sequence.

## ⚙️ Plan Settings (FROZEN — set at plan start, read before any phase)

| Setting | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Mode** | `AUTO` | Orchestrator auto-clears Gates A-C only on **positive, presence-based evidence** (see `plan-lifecycle.md` § AUTO Gate Evidence Contract) — Gate D always halts. |
| **Agents** | `SINGLE` | Fast plan; inline personas, Group C collapses to a self-review checkpoint, Gate C `N/A`. |

> **Frozen config — read before executing ANY phase.** Mutable runtime state lives ONLY in the bottom `## 📍 State & Gates` section.
>
> **Preset provenance:** written by `ptp-parcel-fast` at claim time from the `@sprint-run` batch host's locked preset (`Mode=AUTO`, `Agents=SINGLE`); the selection question is skipped. Frozen thereafter.

## 1 Phase 1: Expansion & Scoping
**Skill Executed:** `ptp-context-hunter`
**Intent:**
Make every AUTO gate-clear require positive, presence-based evidence instead of the absence of a rejection, so a vague or empty self-review can no longer pass by omission.

**In Scope:**
- The AUTO-clear test for Gate A, Gate B, and the inline Phase 6 self-review.
- One written **evidence contract** per gate, stated once and cited elsewhere (no restatement).
- Template evidence shapes: Phase 4 acceptance criteria, the Phase 6 self-review row-per-criterion, the Phase 9 command + exit-code table.
- `sprint-run` § 5: an unproven gate-critical section becomes a stop-the-line cause, not just a `REJECTED` verdict.
- The `base-context.md` line describing AUTO ("after mechanical verification") if its wording must change — with the prefix `-Sync` cascade that implies.

**Out of Scope:**
- The meaning of the gates, or the human Gate D.
- The claim-eligibility predicate (that is T1-E3.09).
- The `MULTI`/`SINGLE` gate set, or adding an independent reviewer to `SINGLE`.

**Perimeter Notes:**
- The `SINGLE` self-review is written by the same agent that authored the plan. This parcel cannot make it *independent* — only make its *absence* detectable.
- The evidence test must be mechanical (presence + reference), never a quality judgement, or it will produce false halts.
