## Problem

### G1 — `.devops/sprints/**` is outside the UTF-8 gate

`scripts/check-utf8-agents.ps1:20` scans `.devops\plans`, `archive`, `backlog`, `logs`, `rules`, `templates` — but **not** `.devops\sprints`. Sprint-queue plans are therefore the only plan-state artefact with no encoding coverage.

The gap is live, not theoretical: `@sprint-plan` moved three plans out of `.devops/backlog/` and into `.devops/sprints/sprint-8-.../`, and the scan count fell **218 → 216** on that commit (`-3` moved out, `+1` `SPRINTS.md` added). Any plan in a queue can now carry mojibake or a stray BOM into the batch runner's context, which is exactly the class of corruption the gate exists to catch (`check-utf8-agents.ps1` was hardened for CP437 mojibake in machinery 33).

**Fix:** add `.devops\sprints` to the directory loop on line 20. One entry; no logic change.

### G2 — `@sprint-plan` does not test *mutual* `touches` overlap across the set it commits

`sprint-run` evaluates the `touches`-overlap predicate **per claim**, and `plan-lifecycle.md` § Claim Protocol applies it again at claim time. Neither `@sprint-plan` § 4 (Boundary Check) nor § 6 (Move Committed Plans) tests the committed set **against itself** before committing.

The consequence is a queue that looks healthy and can never batch. Sprint 8 committed three plans whose `touches` sets are mutually overlapping (all three edit `.devops/agents/**`, `base-context*`, `sync-manifest.yaml`, `version-history.md`, `HOW-TO.md`). A single `@sprint-run` therefore claims the first plan and records the other two as skips — `T1-E3.03` on the unmet-`depends_on` clause, `T1-E3.05` on the overlap clause — so "run the sprint" silently delivers a third of the sprint. Sprint 8 only delivered in full because the operator explicitly agreed to three serial waves with a wrap-up between each, which is a human workaround for a mechanical check.

**Fix:** a preflight step in `@sprint-plan` that runs the same normalization + prefix-overlap test from `sprint-run` § 2 across the candidate set, and reports the resulting **wave decomposition** before the commit. A queue that needs N waves should say so at planning time, when trimming or reordering is still cheap — not at claim time, after the plans have moved. Also state the accepted cost explicitly: N waves means N Gate D verdicts and N wrap-ups, not one consolidated verdict.

### G3 — the close-of-sprint hygiene scan cannot run in a repo without `src/`

`@sprint-close` § 2 is a mandatory ritual step: scan the files touched this sprint and promote anything over threshold into `REFACTORING.md`'s Kill List. The scanner it calls, `scripts/spaghetti-monster-scan.cjs`, hard-codes its walk root:

```
Error: ENOENT: no such file or directory, scandir '...\pass-the-parcel\src'
    at walk (scripts/spaghetti-monster-scan.cjs:6:26)
```

It has **no `src/`-absent guard**, unlike `scripts/wiki_coverage_check.py`, which detects the missing tree and no-ops cleanly ("no `src/` tree in this workspace — nothing to cover"). So in any workspace with no application source tree — including this template — the hygiene step is unavailable, and the failure is a crash rather than a clear skip.

There is a second, larger problem behind the crash: the scanner's root assumption means it **never sees the machinery it is meant to police**. This repo's executable surface is `scripts/*.ps1`/`*.py`/`*.cjs`, `.devops/skills/**`, `.devops/agents/**`, and `.devops/templates/**` — none of it under `src/`. A satellite with an app would get the app scanned and its machinery ignored, which is backwards for a template whose product *is* the machinery.

**Fix:** (a) guard the walk — if the root is absent, print the same one-line no-op the coverage gate prints and exit `0`; (b) add the machinery roots to the scan set (at minimum `scripts/`, `.devops/skills/`, `.devops/agents/`, `.devops/templates/`) with the same thresholds, or state explicitly in `sprint-close` § 2 that the scan is app-source-scoped and hand off the machinery pass to `@spaghetti-monster`. Do not silently skip the step either way.

**Second half of G3 — the refactoring lane has no live register.** `.devops/backlog/REFACTORING.md` **does not exist** in this repo; only its seed `.devops/templates/REFACTORING.template.md` does. So even with the scanner fixed, `@sprint-close` § 2's promotion step ("add it to the REFACTORING.md Kill List") and § 6's "Ensure REFACTORING.md reflects the scan results" have no target, and `@sprint-plan` § 3's "Kill List" source is empty by construction. The lane is dormant. **Decide and record:** either adopt the register (seed → `.devops/backlog/REFACTORING.md`, as `SPRINTS.md` was adopted at Sprint 8 planning), or state in `sprint-close`/`sprint-plan` that the lane is opt-in and what to do when it is absent. Until then, the hygiene ritual's output has nowhere to land.
