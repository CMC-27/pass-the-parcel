# Backlog: T1-E2.12 - F2's close-out CI read has no checker (and the no-push window has no home)

## Theme-Epic: T1 - Parcel Pipeline Machinery, E2 - Governance & Transport Integrity

It is the first thing this template has ever had that looks at the *remote* CI result at close-out, and it is exactly what the four-day window needed. It is also, by construction, unenforced.

## Findings

| # | Tension | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The step has no deterministic check.** Q5 rejected a `scripts/ci_status.py` on the explicit ground that a network/`gh` read must not enter the portable surface (`machinery-gates.yml`'s no-secret/no-wiring header). That is the *same* outcome class the parcel was raised to remove — narrowed, not closed. |
| 2 | **The no-push window has nowhere to live.** `T1-E2.11` `Q8` ruled a push-cadence rule **out of scope**: F2 makes a red *result* visible, a cadence rule would make the *absence* of a result impossible. They are different requirements, and the operator recorded the recurrence without parking it. Phase 7 `N-PO-2` named the gap: the recurrence has no backlog home, so it would evaporate at wrap-up. | The proximate cause of the four-day latency — nobody pushed, so nothing was ever checked — stays unaddressed and unrecorded. `T1-E2.11` Phase 7's judgement is the honest statement of the limit: it ends the *locally-green/remotely-red* class, not the *nobody-looked* class. |
| 3 | **The Windows-local run is still the last real gate.** Phase 7 `C-R3-PO-3`: on a host-sensitivity fix, CI green alone is not the whole contract — the host that was *already* green is where regressions would hide. `T1-E2.11` proves both hosts at one commit, but nothing states the two-host obligation as a standing rule. | The specific bug is fixed; the *class* ("a host-sensitive gate whose other host nobody runs") is not. |

## Boundary

- **Do not re-open `T1-E3.22`.** That item owns the **claim-time** green baseline (is the trunk green *before* work starts?). This item owns the **close-out** read (was it green when work finished?) and the cadence question. `T1-E2.11` `CW6` required these stay disjoint — two dialects of "green baseline" is the defect, not the fix.
- **Do not move the read into the portable workflow.** `machinery-gates.yml`'s no-secret/no-wiring header is a Q7-ratified invariant; `T1-E2.11` `Q7` answered *no shift*.
- A cadence rule collides with `@test-and-deploy` § 5 (*"You MUST ask the user for explicit permission before pushing. Never push automatically"*). Any cadence work must reconcile that deliberately, not route around it.
